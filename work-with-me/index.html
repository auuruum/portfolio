How it works / 02

A conversation.
Then a clear plan.

You bring the idea. I ask the technical questions. We agree what gets built, what it costs and when it’s ready.

  1. 01

    Tell me the idea

    A few sentences, a sketch or a reference. Start with what you want it to do.

    In your own words
  2. 02

    Agree the details

    We work out the scope, price, timeline, deliverables and payment schedule before starting.

    A quote for your project
  3. 03

    Build & review

    I build the agreed work and show you the progress. Larger projects can have separate milestones.

    Something you can check
  4. 04

    Test & hand over

    We check the agreed result, settle the remaining payment and hand over the files and instructions.

    Ready for your next step
↳

Every project is quoted individually. If the idea grows along the way, we agree the extra work and its price before adding it.

A few useful details

Before you ask.

How much will it cost?+

The price depends on what you need built, what already exists and what has to work together. After discussing it, I’ll give you a quote for the agreed scope.

Do I need a technical specification?+

You can start with an idea, an example or a problem you want solved. I’ll ask about the technical details we need to work out.

How does payment work?+

We confirm the payment method and schedule in the proposal. An advance and remaining payment, or separately paid milestones, can be agreed depending on the project.

What happens after delivery?+

We agree the handover, source code, instructions and any period for fixing issues before starting. Ongoing hosting, maintenance and new features can be discussed separately.
